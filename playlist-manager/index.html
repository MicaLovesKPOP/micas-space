Mica YouTube Playlist Manager

A locally operated personal utility for organising the owner's existing YouTube and YouTube Music playlists and assembling historically researched music collections.

What the application does

  • Read playlist contents and publicly available video information through YouTube Data API Services.
  • Prepare ordered playlist imports, remove duplicates when expressly authorised, and verify that the resulting playlist matches its intended tracks and order.
  • Create and maintain specifically authorised playlists, including historically researched Dutch Top 40 collections. Historical rankings are based on independent chart records, not YouTube viewing statistics.
  • Review alternative recordings and help identify unavailable or unsuitable tracks without downloading YouTube audio or video.

Access and control

The application is intended solely for its owner. It is not an online service, public download or sign-in portal. The desktop program uses Google's OAuth consent flow, identifies the authorised channel, and limits account changes to the owner's explicit instructions. Playlists may be private or unlisted as instructed; an unlisted link can be shared by its recipients.

This website is documentation only. It does not request Google credentials or access your YouTube account. Please read the privacy policy and terms of use before authorising the desktop application.
